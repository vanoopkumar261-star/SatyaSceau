How it works

Four steps, no app to install

Register & print your QR

Choose Personal UPI or Company, name your shop, print the QR.

Customer uploads

They scan your QR and upload the original payment screenshot. No login.

Your bank confirms

Your bank's credit SMS is forwarded automatically, or you paste it.

Instant verdict

Green, amber or red, spoken aloud on both phones, with a signed record.

✅ Verified UTR + amount match your bank, first use ⏳ Waiting looks fine, bank SMS not here yet ❌ Not verified replayed, edited, or no credit in 5 min

Built to be trusted

What happens behind the green tick

🔢

UTR matching

The 12-digit UPI reference is identical on both sides of a payment, so we match on that, not on app IDs.

🛡️

Replay protection

Each UTR can be used once, enforced by the database. Lookalike and re-saved screenshots are caught.

🔊

Hindi & English voice

The verdict is spoken on the merchant's and the customer's phone. No reading needed at a busy counter.

✍️

Signed records

Every verdict becomes a write-once record signed with Ed25519. Anyone can verify it, and edits break it.

🔒

Privacy first

Screenshots are never stored. Only the UTR, amount and time are kept, deleted after 7 days, and only with your consent.

📱

Any phone

Works in the browser on any smartphone. Bank SMS from official sender IDs only.
